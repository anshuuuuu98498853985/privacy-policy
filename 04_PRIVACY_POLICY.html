Information the app accesses

The inspected LedgeRush release is an offline single-player parkour game. It does not request Android runtime permissions. The project contains a simulated speech-provider interface, but the inspected Android manifest has no microphone permission; verify the final scene wiring before release.

Information stored on the device

The game stores level progress and unlocks, Panda companion unlock state, audio preferences, and customized mobile control positions and sizes in private app configuration files on the device. The inspected game does not transmit these saves to the publisher. Settings' Reset Progress action resets active progression but first retains a copy of the previous save in a private backup file. To remove both active and backup copies, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app through Android.

Information collected or transmitted

In the inspected source and release bundle, we found no account registration, analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, payment, or backend service integration. The merged release manifest declares no Android permissions, including INTERNET or AD_ID. We therefore found no app-originated collection or transmission in this build. Revisit this statement if the app, dependencies, or Play configuration changes.

Network communication and third-party services

The app does not embed a YouTube player. The current build offers user-selected external links to YouTube and Instagram and a link to Godot Engine notices. If you choose a link, Android opens the destination in an external app or browser. The destination provider and network operator may process connection information, such as your IP address, under their own policies. The publisher does not control those services.

Sharing, security, retention and deletion

The inspected game does not share local save data with the publisher or a third-party SDK. Save data remains in private app storage until reset, app storage is cleared, or the app is uninstalled. Android private app storage and operating-system protections are used; the game does not implement separate save-file encryption. This policy does not make claims about device-level backup behavior outside the app's control.
